Premium
Pick any open level in the bank, see its difficulty, and skip to the next bank after 3 wins. On by default during the beta — the purchase comes later.
Mix modes
After a win, the next level comes from the other mode — Survive, then Lose, then Survive…
Tutorial
Guided intro for Survive, Lose or Duel — pick one, or all three.
Feel
Calm mode
No shake, no combo words, no shards — clears just fade. For a quieter game.
Theme
Night, Paper (light) or Wood.
Colour-safe blocks
Each piece colour also carries a small mark, for colour-blind play.
Rewind after a loss
After a fail, offer to jump back to the last move that still had a solution. Off: start over from move 1 and earn it. A rewound win never gets the Perfect badge.
Haptics
A tick on place, a thump on clear (phones only).
Duel
Premoves
While the other side is on move, drop pieces to queue them (shown see-through). They play in order when your turn comes, if they still fit. Tap a queued piece to cancel them all.
